What makes first-time investors stop their SIP?

This engine read public conversations about SIPs (app reviews, YouTube comments, Reddit and TradingQ&A) and tagged each one against a fixed codebook of 19 questions, then linked the tags to the metric: the share of first-time investors with an active SIP 12 months after starting.

The codebook: 19 questions asked of every item

The AI may only answer with these options, must quote the words behind each answer, and leaves a question out when the text doesn't say. Prompt v1.5. The check under each question is its score on the 20-item hand-labelled golden set.

Rules applied after tagging (code, not AI)

ScopeMutual fund SIP when "Kind of SIP" is MF explicit or MF inferred. Stock, ETF or gold SIPs are kept separate; unclear items stay out of the main counts.

Metric treeFrom "What they did" (and the trigger for cancels and failed payments): Still continuing, increased, reduced or changed date → Never lapsed. Skipped → Lapsed → Chose to lapse → Skip. Severity tier1 Lapsed (cancelled, stopped, auto-cancelled, moved app); 2 Missed (skipped, payment failed); 3 Reduced or withdrew; 4 Considering stopping; 5 Worried, no action. An item takes its most severe tier.

How the engine works

AI is used only where meaning has to be read (steps 3 and 4). Everything else is fixed code, so it is cheap, repeatable and auditable. A human check closed every step.

  1. CollectcodePlay Store reviews for Groww and 5 competitors since Jan 2023 (about 295,000), App Store reviews, 59,394 comments on 93 finance videos, 9,752 TradingQ&A posts, 1,406 Reddit items. Public data only; no usernames.
  2. Content checkcodeRemoves empty and generic items and labels replies prompted by a creator's call to action. Every exclusion keeps a reason; nothing is deleted.
  3. RelevanceAIFive models tested on 100 hand-labelled items; Claude Haiku 5.5 won on recall within budget. Blind audit: recall about 80%. Result: 35,796 relevant plus 3,167 uncertain.
  4. Tag with the codebookAI19 questions with fixed options and a quoted clue for every answer. Prompt refined five times on a practice set, never on the golden set. Cost $8.14 for 38,963 items.
  5. DerivecodeFixed rules add scope, voice, metric tree position, severity tier and path, as shown in the codebook section.
  6. Quantify and rankcode + judgementShares per source, never pooled. Opportunities pass four gates, then sit on a frequency by severity grid. Tested in user interviews.

Target chosen: the first tight month in a young salaried first-timer's first year, when the SIP date collides with a cash shortfall. The engine shows it is common and severe; interviews showed why: the break is temporary and cash-driven, yet it counts as a lapse.
